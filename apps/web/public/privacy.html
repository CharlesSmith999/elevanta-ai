Privacy and Gmail access

Updated October 1, 2026

What the Gmail connection does

An authorized Admin connects a Gmail mailbox using Google's consent screen. Elevanta AI requests read-only access to email messages and settings. The integration uses that permission to find and read qualifying Bark lead emails in the Inbox or Spam folder and put their lead information into the Marketing research queue. The integration does not send email or modify Gmail messages or labels.

What is stored

The CRM stores parsed lead information, original request details, message identifiers, receipt times, ingestion outcomes and research activity. It also stores the connected mailbox address and encrypted authorization credentials so scheduled checks can run without repeated sign-in. An Admin starts the intake window; same-mailbox reconnection resumes that window.

Who can access information

Admins and Marketing users can access the research queue. Sales users cannot access that queue. When Marketing hands a researched lead to Sales, the assigned salesperson can see the contact information and handoff information provided for that lead. Mailbox connection settings are Admin-only.

Service providers and protection

Gmail access is provided by Google. CRM records are stored in Supabase, and the application and its server run on Vercel. OAuth tokens are encrypted in server-side storage and are not exposed as browser configuration. Access is controlled by the CRM's role and workspace permissions.

Control, retention and requests

You can revoke Elevanta AI's access in your Google Account's third-party connections. Revocation stops future authorized Gmail access; it does not automatically delete leads already imported into the CRM. An Admin can also disable intake. Records remain in the CRM until removed through its data-management process. Contact the operator for questions or a deletion request. This notice does not promise a fixed retention period or immediate deletion.
