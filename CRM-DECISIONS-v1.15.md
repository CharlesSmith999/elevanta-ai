# CRM Decisions v1.15: Focused lead and research workflows

Approved by the product owner: proceed with the UX refinement documented in [the screen contract](docs/LEAD-UX-REFINEMENT-PROPOSAL.md).

Creation uses details followed by review and routing, with repeatable contact methods. Lead details prioritize contacts and next action. Research separates editing from the final Sales handoff review. Admin mailbox configuration is collapsed away from routine research. Existing permissions, immediate assignment, history, duplicate and DNC protections remain authoritative.

This extends v1.14. Database changes, if needed for atomic multiple-contact creation, must be deployed before dependent application changes. Completion requires build and workflow verification; production release must be recorded separately.
