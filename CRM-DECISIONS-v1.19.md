# Lead detail shortcuts, v1.19

Approved by the owner on October 14, 2026: restore the previous Leads page and add only Copy, Dial and Email shortcuts on the existing lead detail page.

This supersedes the Sales Workbench UI decision in v1.18. Restore the prior list and detail navigation, remove the split-panel workbench and its inventory endpoint, and retain all existing lead data and permissions.

Copy is available beside displayed contact methods. Dial opens the configured phone application; Email opens the configured email application. These links never record an activity or claim that contact happened. Invalid, removed, incorrect, wrong-person or do-not-contact methods must not receive outreach links. Real contact health must load before outreach links appear.

No WhatsApp, Save and Next, new lead layout, schema change or data migration is included.
